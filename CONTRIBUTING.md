# Suggesting changes to the MPATW Wiki

This wiki is written and maintained by **ImP4ncake**, and it doesn't take pull requests from other people.

To suggest a change or an addition (a mistake, something out of date, a new code, a screenshot, an idea for a page), send a message on Discord to **`imp4ncake`**. The page [Suggest a Change](https://imp4ncake.github.io/mpatw-wiki/wiki/suggest/) explains how.

Thanks for helping keep the wiki accurate!

# Badges

Almost every badge also unlocks a **title** and a **tag effect** (an emoji) that you can show off. Badges are shared between the standard and the [18+ version](versions.md) of the game.

This page **updates automatically** from the official Roblox data: new badges and the number of players who own each one appear here on their own.

??? info "How the rarity is calculated"

    Rarity is calculated by the wiki: it compares how many players own a badge with how many players own the **Welcome** badge (everyone who ever joined the game).

    | Rarity | Owned by |
    |---|---|
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--common">Common</span> | 25% of players or more |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span> | 2% to 25% |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--rare">Rare</span> | 0.2% to 2% |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--epic">Epic</span> | 0.01% to 0.2% |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--legendary">Legendary</span> | Less than 0.01% |

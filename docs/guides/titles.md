# How to Unlock Titles

Titles are unlocked with **badges**: earn the badge and its title becomes available, usually together with a tag effect.

1. Find the title you want on the [Titles](../badges/titles.md) page.
2. Click it to see which badge unlocks it and how to get that badge.
3. Follow the [Badge Hunting Guide](badge-hunting.md) to plan which badges to get first.

# Beginner's Guide

New to the 18+ version? Here is everything you need for your first hour.

## 1. Join a server

Press **Play** on the main screen. Under the buttons you can see the server's **primary language** and the countries its players come from: look for a server in a language you speak. Prefer text chat? Use the [No-Voice Servers](../servers/no-voice.md).

## 2. Look around

Every player shows their country flag, their [title](../badges/titles.md) and their [reputation](../mechanics/reputation.md). Say hi to people from different countries: that's the point of the game!

## 3. Get your first badges

These are quick and easy:

| Badge | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Pet The Cat | Find the cat and pet it |
| Pet The Dog | Find the dog and pet it |
| Pianist | Play the piano |
| Builder | Build 10 blocks with the Hammer |

Each one unlocks a [title and a tag effect](../badges/titles.md).

## 4. Earn coins

Coins arrive regularly while you play. Redeem [codes](../mechanics/codes.md) and visit the **AFK World** when you step away. More in [How to Earn Coins](earn-coins.md).

## 5. Explore the activities

Draw, play chess, try the lottery or fight in the arenas: see [Activities & Locations](../activities/index.md).

!!! warning "Stay safe"

    Never share personal information such as your address, phone number or passwords. Use Roblox's block and report tools if someone makes you uncomfortable: see [Reporting Players](../community/reporting.md).

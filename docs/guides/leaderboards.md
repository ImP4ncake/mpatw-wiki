# Leaderboard Guide

<p class="mp-lead">The game has <b>leaderboards</b> that rank the players. Reaching the top of one gives one of the rarest badges in the game, and so does losing it.</p>

## Good to know

<div class="mp-facts" markdown>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--gold" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-trophy:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>Reach #1</b>Be #1 on a non-timed leaderboard (one that never resets) to get the **Angel** badge, its title and the 🥇 tag effect.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--red" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-trending-down:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>Lose it, get a badge</b>If someone takes your #1 spot while the leaderboard updates in the game, you get the **Fallen Angel** badge and the 🥀 tag effect.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--blue" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-refresh-cw:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>They update while you play</b>Leaderboards refresh from time to time in the game, so your spot can change while you are online.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--violet" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-shield-check:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>Play fair</b>Faking donations or using a bug for infinite coins or reputation gets you banned and data wiped: see the rules below.</span></div>
</div>

# How to Earn Coins

All the known ways to earn [coins](../mechanics/coins.md), from easiest to slowest.

1. **Redeem codes.** Quick and free: see [Codes](../mechanics/codes.md).
2. **Invite a friend.** The **Invite a Friend!** badge gives 100 coins.
3. **Just play.** Coins arrive regularly while you are in the game: the timer next to your balance shows when.
4. **Use the AFK World.** Going away for a while? Press **AFK World** on the main screen and keep earning. Earning 1,000 coins there also gives the **AFK** badge.
5. **Triple Coins.** The **Triple Coins** [gamepass](../items/gamepasses.md) triples the coins you earn from most sources.

!!! note

    Coins don't carry over between the 18+ and the standard version.

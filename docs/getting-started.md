# Getting started

New to *Meet People Across The World*? This page covers the basics.

## Joining the game

1. Open the [game page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/78699621133325) and press **Play**.
2. You can play on computer, phone, tablet, console or in VR. Joining in VR at least once gives you the **Played in VR** badge.
3. Your first visit gives you the **Welcome to Meet People Across The World!** badge.

!!! tip "Voice or no voice?"

    The game has both **voice chat servers** and **no voice chat servers**, so you can choose whether you want to talk out loud or only use text chat. See [Servers](features/servers.md).

## Your first minutes

- **Check out other players.** Every player's country is shown, which is the whole idea of the game: meeting people from different parts of the world. See [Country](sections/country.md).
- **Set up your profile.** You can customize how your profile looks.
- **Be friendly.** Your [reputation](features/reputation-and-comments.md) is visible to everyone, and people can leave comments on your profile.
- **Explore the map.** There are lots of [activities](features/activities.md) to try, like the piano, the drawing area and the arenas.

## Earning your first coins

Coins are the main in-game currency. A few easy ways to start:

- **Redeem codes.** Open the menu (the four squares icon on the left of the screen), go to **Settings** and type a code into the code box. See [Codes](economy/codes.md) for the current list.
- **Invite a friend.** The **Invite a Friend!** badge rewards you with a title, a tag and 100 coins.
- **Visit the AFK World.** You can earn coins there while you are away from the keyboard.

More details on [Currencies](economy/currencies.md).

## Easy badges to start with

These badges are quick to get and a good way to explore the game:

| Badge | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Pet The Cat | Find the cat and pet it |
| Pet The Dog | Find the dog and pet it |
| Pianist | Play the piano |
| Builder | Build 10 blocks with the Hammer |

The full list is on the [Badges](badges.md) page.

## Things to know

- There is a separate **18+ version** of the game. Gamepasses and badges are shared between the two versions, but the rest of your saved data is not. See [Standard vs 18+ version](versions.md).
- The game's rules are posted in its official community server. Read them before you start chatting.

# Draw Area

In the **Draw Area** you can draw with other players. Drawing **1,000 times** gives the **Artist** badge.

--8<-- "generated/badge/artist.md"

!!! warning "In the rules"

    Everything you draw counts as an **Image** in the [Rules](../community/rules.md). Suggestive drawings break [c9](../community/rules.md#c9){ .mp-rule }, NSFW drawings [d1](../community/rules.md#d1){ .mp-rule }, and scribbling nonsense only to make people lag [c6](../community/rules.md#c6){ .mp-rule }. Drawing together with someone who breaks the rules is [a6](../community/rules.md#a6){ .mp-rule }.

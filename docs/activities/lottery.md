# Lottery

The **Lottery** lets you roll a number. Rolling **777** gives the **Win the Lottery** badge.

--8<-- "generated/badge/win-the-lottery.md"

During **St. Patrick's Day** there is a special lottery: rolling **7777** gave the limited **Blessed** badge. See [St. Patrick's Day](../events/st-patricks.md).

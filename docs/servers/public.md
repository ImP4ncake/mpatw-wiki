## Joining a server

Press **Play** on the main screen. Under the buttons the game shows the **current server**: its number, how many players are in it, its primary language and the countries most of its players come from. The **Servers** button opens the server list, so you can pick another one.

## Voice chat servers

The main place, **18+ Meet People Across The World**, has the **voice chat servers**, where you talk to people out loud with Roblox voice chat.

!!! note

    Voice chat on Roblox depends on your account being eligible for it. Check Roblox's own help pages for the current requirements.

Voice changers are **free** now: the old **Voice Changers** gamepass isn't sold anymore. Players who bought it have the **Vocalist** [title](../badges/titles.md#team-and-special-titles), which can't be got anymore.

## No-voice servers

The separate place **NO-VOICECHAT 18+ Meet People Across The World** has servers where nobody uses voice chat. It's the best choice if you prefer text chat or can't use voice.

## The 18+ version

The 18+ version is a separate Roblox experience, only for adults. It was created on **18 October 2024**, about seven weeks after the standard version, and has more than **33 million visits**. Players stay longer here: about **19 minutes** per visit on average, compared with about 10.5 in the standard version.

According to the game's description, **gamepasses and badges** save between the 18+ and the standard version, while everything else (for example coins) does not. This hasn't been confirmed in-game yet: the full comparison is on [Game Versions](../game-info/versions.md).

!!! warning "Adults only, still Roblox"

    The 18+ label doesn't change Roblox's rules: the [Roblox Community Standards](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards) and the game's own [rules](../community/rules.md) still apply.

!!! info "In the rules"

    What you play or say in voice chat counts as **Audio** and **Say** in the [Rules](../community/rules.md). Explicit audios and moaning break [a2](../community/rules.md#a2){ .mp-rule }. An advertisement in voice chat is fine ([c4](../community/rules.md#c4){ .mp-rule }), and playing on another account while you are banned from voice chat is not ban evasion ([e3](../community/rules.md#e3){ .mp-rule }).

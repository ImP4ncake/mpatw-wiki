# 18+ Version

The **18+ version** is a separate Roblox experience, only for adults. It was created on **18 October 2024**, about seven weeks after the standard version, and has more than **33 million visits**.

- To join, your Roblox account must meet Roblox's requirements for 18+ experiences.
- **Gamepasses and badges** carry over between the 18+ and the standard version. Everything else you have saved (for example coins) does not.
- Players stay longer here: about **19 minutes** per visit on average, compared with about 10.5 in the standard version.

The full comparison is on [Game Versions](../game-info/versions.md).

!!! warning "Adults only, still Roblox"

    The 18+ label doesn't change Roblox's rules: the [Roblox Community Standards](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards) and the game's own [rules](../community/rules.md) still apply.

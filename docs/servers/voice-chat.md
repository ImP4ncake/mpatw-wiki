# Voice Chat Servers

The main place of the 18+ version has **voice chat servers**, where you can talk to people out loud with Roblox voice chat. When you join, the main screen shows the current server's number, how many players are in it (up to 65), its **primary language** and the countries its players come from.

!!! note

    Voice chat on Roblox depends on your account being eligible for it. Check Roblox's own help pages for the current requirements.

The **Voice Changers** gamepass added voice effects. It is not for sale at the moment.

Prefer text only? Use the [No-Voice Servers](no-voice.md).

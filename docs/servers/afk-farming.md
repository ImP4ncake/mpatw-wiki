# AFK Farming Server

<p class="mp-lead" markdown="span">Going away for a while? The **AFK World** is a separate server where you keep earning [coins](../mechanics/coins.md) while you are away from the keyboard.</p>

<div class="mp-facts mp-facts--three" markdown>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--blue" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-mouse-pointer-click:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>How to get there</b>Press **AFK World** on the main screen, next to **Play** and **Servers**.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--gold" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-coins:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>Coins while away</b>Your coins keep coming while you don't play.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--violet" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-award:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>A badge for it</b>Earning **1,000 coins** in the AFK World gives the **AFK** badge.</span></div>
</div>

## The AFK badge

--8<-- "generated/badge/afk.md"

It also unlocks the **AFK** [title](../badges/titles.md#badge-titles) and the 💤 tag effect.

## More ways to earn

The AFK World is one of the ways to farm coins: the others, from codes to the **Triple Coins** gamepass, are in [How to Farm Coins](../guides/earn-coins.md). Saving up **100,000 coins** gives the **Golden** [title](../badges/titles.md#coins-and-robux).

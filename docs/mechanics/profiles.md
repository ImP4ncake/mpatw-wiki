# Player Profiles

Every player has a profile that other players can open.

## What players can see

| Element | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Country flag** | Your [country](countries.md) |
| **Title** | Shown above your name. Titles are unlocked with [badges](../badges/titles.md) |
| **Tag effect** | An emoji effect, also unlocked with [badges](../badges/tag-effects.md) |
| **Reputation** | Your [reputation](reputation.md) score, shown under your name. Other players give it to you with their coins |
| **Comments** | What other players wrote on your profile: see [Comments](comments.md) |

## Customization

The **CUSTOMIZE** button in the menu lets you change how your profile looks. The **Better Customization** [gamepass](../items/gamepasses.md) unlocks extra options.

!!! info "In the rules"

    Your age or your social media in your bio are fine ([a9](../community/rules.md#a9){ .mp-rule }). A username or display name that breaks a rule is a ban for [e1](../community/rules.md#e1){ .mp-rule }.

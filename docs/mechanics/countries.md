# Countries

The core idea of the game is that **everyone's country is visible**: each player is shown with their country's flag, so you always know where the people you are talking to come from.

## Discoveries

The **DISCOVERIES** button in the menu tracks the countries you have discovered. There are **250 countries**, and discovering all of them gives the **Explorer** badge, one of the rarest in the game:

## Servers and languages

When you join, the main screen shows the current server's **primary language** and the countries most of its players come from, so you can find people who speak your language.

## Related

- **Country Trivia** is a quiz about countries: see [Country Trivia](../activities/country-trivia.md).
- The **Custom Country** gamepass lets players set any country as their origin. It is not for sale at the moment.

# Reputation

<p class="mp-lead"><b>Reputation</b> is a score that other players give you. They spend their coins on it, and it can go up or down.</p>

<div class="mp-rate" markdown>
<div class="mp-rate__row" markdown="span"><span class="mp-rate__pill mp-rate__pill--coin">:lucide-coins: 1 coin</span><span class="mp-rate__arrow">:lucide-arrow-right:</span><span class="mp-rate__choice"><span class="mp-rate__pill mp-rate__pill--up">:lucide-thumbs-up: +1</span><span class="mp-rate__or">or</span><span class="mp-rate__pill mp-rate__pill--down">:lucide-thumbs-down: −1</span></span></div>
<div class="mp-rate__note" markdown="span">Every coin spent gives a player **1 reputation**, positive or negative.</div>
</div>

## Where you see it

Your reputation is the big number on your [profile](profiles.md) card, under your picture. It is also shown under your name in the game, together with your [title](../badges/titles.md).

## How to give reputation

<div class="mp-steps" markdown>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-coins: Collect **coins** by playing: see [Coins](coins.md)</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-users: Meet and talk to **other players**</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-thumbs-up: Give them **+** or **−** reputation, and leave a [comment](comments.md) on their profile</div>
</div>

## Good to know

- **You choose** who gets your reputation, and whether it is positive or negative.
- **Your coins are the only limit.** With **1,000 coins** you can give other players a total of **+1,000** or **−1,000** reputation.
- **There is no cooldown**: you can give reputation whenever you want, to the same player too.
- **It can go below zero**, even very far below, like in the picture above.

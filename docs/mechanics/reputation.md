# Reputation

Every player has a **reputation** score. It is shown under your name in the game, together with your [title](../badges/titles.md), and on your [profile](profiles.md).

You don't build reputation on your own: it comes from the **players you interact with**. They turn their [coins](coins.md) into reputation for you, and you can do the same for them.

## How it works

<div class="mp-steps" markdown>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-coins: Collect **coins** by playing: see [Coins](coins.md)</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-users: Meet and talk to **other players**</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-thumbs-up: Turn your coins into **+** or **−** reputation for them, and leave a [comment](comments.md) on their profile</div>
</div>

- **You choose** who gets your reputation, and whether it is positive or negative.
- **Your coins are the limit.** For example, a player with **1,000 coins** can give other players a total of **+1,000** or **−1,000** reputation.
- **Reputation can go below zero**, even very far below.

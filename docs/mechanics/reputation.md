# Reputation

<p class="mp-lead"><b>Reputation</b> is a score that other players give you. They spend their coins on it, and it can go up or down.</p>

<div class="mp-rate" markdown>
<div class="mp-rate__row" markdown="span"><span class="mp-rate__pill mp-rate__pill--coin">:lucide-coins: 1 coin</span><span class="mp-rate__arrow">:lucide-arrow-right:</span><span class="mp-rate__choice"><span class="mp-rate__pill mp-rate__pill--up">:lucide-thumbs-up: +1</span><span class="mp-rate__or">or</span><span class="mp-rate__pill mp-rate__pill--down">:lucide-thumbs-down: −1</span></span></div>
<div class="mp-rate__note" markdown="span">Every coin spent gives a player **1 reputation**, positive or negative.</div>
</div>

## How to give reputation

<div class="mp-steps" markdown>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-coins: Collect **coins** by playing: see [Coins](coins.md)</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-users: Meet and talk to **other players**</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-thumbs-up: Give them **+** or **−** reputation, and leave a [comment](comments.md) on their profile</div>
</div>

## Good to know

<div class="mp-facts" markdown>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--violet" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-user-check:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>You choose</b>Who gets your reputation, and whether it is positive or negative.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--gold" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-coins:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>Your coins are the only limit</b>With 1,000 coins you can give a total of +1,000 or −1,000.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--blue" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-timer-off:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>No cooldown</b>Give reputation whenever you want, to the same player too.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--red" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-trending-down:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>It can go below zero</b>Even very far below, like the −500,976 in the picture.</span></div>
</div>

!!! warning "In the rules"

    Getting or giving reputation without it costing coins, through a bug, gets you banned **and data wiped** ([c3](../community/rules.md#c3){ .mp-rule }).

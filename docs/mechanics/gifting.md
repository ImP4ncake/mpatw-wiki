# Gifting

You can give things to other players as gifts.

- Some items in the shop have a **Gift** button, for example the limited **Pumpkin Tip Jar** (see [Limited Items](../items/limited-items.md)).
- The **Valentine's Pack** could be bought or received as a gift during Valentine's Day.

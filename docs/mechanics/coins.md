# Coins

**Coins** are the main in-game currency. Your balance is shown at the top of the screen, next to a timer that shows when your next coins arrive while you play (for example **+18 in 9m 55s**).

!!! warning "Coins stay in their version"

    Coins don't carry over between the 18+ and the standard version: each one keeps its own balance.

## Ways to earn coins

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Playing** | You receive coins regularly while you are in the game |
| **AFK World** | Earn coins while you are away (button on the main screen) |
| **[Codes](codes.md)** | Some codes give coins |
| **Invite a Friend!** | The badge gives **100 coins** |
| **Triple Coins** gamepass | Triples the coins you earn from most sources |

## Spending coins

| Use | How it works |
|---|---|
| **[Give reputation](reputation.md)** | Every coin gives another player **1 reputation**, positive (+1) or negative (−1) |
| **Make it rain** | Shower a currency on other players. Gives the **Rainmaker** badge |

!!! tip "Save up"

    Having **100,000 coins** gives the **Golden** [title](../badges/titles.md#coins-and-robux).

!!! warning "In the rules"

    Using a bug to get infinite coins gets you banned **and data wiped** ([c3](../community/rules.md#c3){ .mp-rule }). Report the bug instead and you get a reward: reporting a **major bug** on the official Discord gives the **Hero** [title](../badges/titles.md#feats).

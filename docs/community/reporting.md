# Report a Player

<p class="mp-lead">Someone breaking the rules or making you uncomfortable? Report them to the game's mods, report them to Roblox, or simply hide them.</p>

## Report to the game's mods

Bans in this game are made by hand: a player is only banned when a mod sees it or someone reports it.

<div class="mp-steps" markdown>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-book-open: Check the [Rules](rules.md): a report leads to a ban only if what they did matches a **bannable example**</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-camera: If you can, keep a **proof**, like a screenshot or a clip</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-flag: Send a **Player Report** on the [official community Discord](https://discord.com/invite/ASRasdGjzA)</div>
</div>

You don't have to report anyone if you don't want to: nobody gets in trouble for not reporting.

## Report to Roblox

You can also report a player with Roblox's own tools. These reports go to Roblox's moderation team, not to the game's mods.

1. Open the **Roblox menu** (the Roblox logo in the top-left corner of the screen).
2. Find the player and choose **Report**, then pick the reason. For voice chat problems, choose the voice option if it is offered.
3. You can also **block** the player, so you no longer see or hear them.

## Found a bug?

Report it on the [official community Discord](https://discord.com/invite/ASRasdGjzA) instead of using it. Reporting a **major bug** gives the **Hero** [title](../badges/titles.md#feats), while using a bug to get infinite coins or reputation is a ban and a data wipe ([c3](rules.md#c3){ .mp-rule }).

## Just want them gone?

!!! tip "Hide Player"

    Being loud, annoying or mean isn't bannable on its own. Instead, open their **Profile Card** (click them, or click them in the Server List) and choose **Hide Player**.

!!! warning "In the rules"

    Lying in a report to get someone banned, or making several fake reports, is bannable ([c8](rules.md#c8){ .mp-rule }). If you think a Mod abused their power, send an **Admin Report** instead of a Player Report.

!!! tip "Stay safe"

    Never share personal information such as your real name, address, phone number or passwords, even with people you trust in the game.

# Reporting Players

If someone breaks the rules or makes you uncomfortable, you can report them with Roblox's built-in tools.

1. Open the **Roblox menu** (the Roblox logo in the top-left corner of the screen).
2. Find the player and choose **Report**, then pick the reason.
3. You can also **block** the player, so you no longer see or hear them.

Reports go to Roblox's moderation team. For problems with voice chat, report the player the same way and choose the voice option if it is offered.

!!! tip

    Never share personal information such as your real name, address, phone number or passwords, even with people you trust in the game.

!!! warning "In the rules"

    Lying in a report to get someone banned, or making several fake reports, is bannable ([c8](../community/rules.md#c8){ .mp-rule }).

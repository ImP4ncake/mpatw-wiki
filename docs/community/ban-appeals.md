# Ban Appeals

<p class="mp-lead">Banned from the game? Here is how to check your ban, ask for the proof and appeal it.</p>

## How to appeal

<div class="mp-steps" markdown>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-search: Find the **ban code** in your ban message (like **c1**) and open it on the [Rules](rules.md)</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-ticket: Open a ticket on the [official community Discord](https://discord.com/invite/ASRasdGjzA) and ask to be unbanned</div>
<div class="mp-step" markdown="span">:lucide-scale: Explain why your **ban proof** doesn't match any bannable example of that ban code</div>
</div>

## Good to know

<div class="mp-facts" markdown>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--blue" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-file-search:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>You can ask for the proof</b>You can always ask for the proof that got you banned, but not who banned you.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--green" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-rotate-ccw:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>Bans can end early</b>Any mod can unban you early if they believe you won't break the rule again (except the bans below).</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--violet" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-handshake:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>The other player can forgive you</b>If you were banned for something you did to another player, you can also be unbanned if they want you to be.</span></div>
<div class="mp-fact mp-tone--gold" markdown="span"><span class="mp-fact__icon">:lucide-users:</span><span class="mp-fact__text"><b>Friends and alts</b>A friend must ask on their own account, verified with the same username. For an alt account, you must prove it's yours.</span></div>
</div>

Mods answer tickets as volunteers, when they aren't busy: be patient.

## Bans with their own unban

| Ban code | How you get unbanned |
|---|---|
| [e1](rules.md#e1){ .mp-rule } Bannable Name | When the bannable name is gone from your username and display name |
| [e2](rules.md#e2){ .mp-rule } Scamming as a Mod | When all your victims are refunded in full |
| [e3](rules.md#e3){ .mp-rule } Ban Evasion | When your original banned account is unbanned |
| [e4](rules.md#e4){ .mp-rule } Self-Bans | When you ask for it with a ticket |
| [e5](rules.md#e5){ .mp-rule } Below 13 years old | Open any ticket to appeal, and you are unbanned straight away |

## Bans that are never lifted

!!! danger "Never unbanned"

    Some cases of [d2](rules.md#d2){ .mp-rule } [d4](rules.md#d4){ .mp-rule } [d5](rules.md#d5){ .mp-rule } [d6](rules.md#d6){ .mp-rule } [d7](rules.md#d7){ .mp-rule } are **never** unbanned, and they count even when they happen outside the game (unless the ban is verifiably false). Open each rule to see exactly which cases, or read the [Unbans](rules.md#rb-unbans) chapter.

## Banned from the Discord?

Instead of a Discord ban you usually get a special role. Only players who broke a "never unbanned" rule, are below 13, or used an alt to get around those bans are banned from the Discord; anyone else who was banned from it before can be unbanned.

!!! info "Think a Mod abused their power?"

    Don't make a Player Report: submit an **Admin Report** on the [official community Discord](https://discord.com/invite/ASRasdGjzA), where you can also read the Mod Rules.

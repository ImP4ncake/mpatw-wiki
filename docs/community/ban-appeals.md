# Ban Appeals

How to appeal a moderation action.

!!! info "How unbans work"

    The [Unbans](rules.md#rb-unbans) chapter of the Rules explains when a ban can end early and which bans are never lifted. Appeals are made with a ticket on the [official community Discord](https://discord.com/invite/ASRasdGjzA).

# Cosmetics

Cosmetics change how you and your profile look. Related menu buttons: **CUSTOMIZE**, **AVATAR** and **EMOTES**.

- The **Better Customization** [gamepass](gamepasses.md) unlocks extra options.
- Cosmetics have [rarities](rarities.md): unlocking a **Fabled** cosmetic gives the **Fabled** badge, and **10** Fabled cosmetics give the **Transcendent** [title](../badges/titles.md#achievements).

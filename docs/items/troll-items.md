# Troll Items

**Troll items** are items you use on other players. You find them in the **ITEMS** menu, together with the other [items](items.md): press **Equip?** to equip one, and the button turns green and says **Equipped**. Unlike the other items, they cost **Troll Tokens** every time you use them.

There are **27 troll items**. Items marked <span class="mp-limited mp-limited--small">KILLS</span> kill the player you use them on.

| Cost | Items |
|---|---|
| <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--uncommon" title="Troll Tokens per use">1 token</span> | 12 items, mostly grabs and lasers |
| <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--rare" title="Troll Tokens per use">2 tokens</span> | Size Changer Laser and Voice Changer Laser |
| <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--epic" title="Troll Tokens per use">3 tokens</span> | The 10 items that kill (Lethal Bomb is used only once) |
| <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--legendary" title="Troll Tokens per use">15 tokens</span> | Handcuffs |

<small>Names, descriptions and costs as shown in the game's ITEMS menu, checked on 12 October 2026.</small>

# Items

Items are things you can hold and use. You open them with the **ITEMS** button in the menu, and the ones you carry appear in the hotbar at the bottom of the screen (up to 15 slots).

In [custom servers](../servers/custom-servers.md), the owner can make all items free for everyone.

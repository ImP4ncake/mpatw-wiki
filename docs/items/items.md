# Items

Items are things you can hold and use. You open them with the **ITEMS** button in the menu, and the ones you carry appear in the hotbar at the bottom of the screen (up to 15 slots). Press **Equip?** to equip an item, and the button turns green and says **Equipped**.

The ITEMS menu has two kinds of items:

| Kind | How you get them |
|---|---|
| **[Troll items](troll-items.md)** | You use them on other players, and every use costs **Troll Tokens** |
| **[Gamepass items](#gamepass-items)** | They come with a [gamepass](gamepasses.md) |

In [custom servers](../servers/custom-servers.md), the owner can make all items free for everyone.

## Gamepass items

In the ITEMS menu they come right after the troll items. Prices are on the [Gamepasses](gamepasses.md) page, which updates automatically.

| Item | What it does | Gamepass |
|---|---|---|
| **Tip Jar** | Lets other players donate Robux to you in game | Tip Jar |
| **Fly** | Lets you fly. Toggle it with **F** | Fly |
| **Friend Grab** | Grab, Lasso, Piggyback or Carry anyone for free, but they have to accept your request | Grab a Friend |
| **Invisibility** | Makes you invisible. Toggle it with **G** | Invisibility |
| **Fusion Coil** | Move much faster and jump much higher | Coil Pack |
| **Speed Coil** | Move much faster | Coil Pack |
| **Gravity Coil** | Jump much higher | Coil Pack |
| **Non-Lethal Shotgun** | A shotgun that can't hurt anyone | [Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack |
| **Non-Lethal Knife** | A knife that can't hurt anyone | [Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack |
| **Non-Lethal Greatsword** | A greatsword that can't hurt anyone. Press **F** to parry | [Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack |
| **Non-Lethal Sniper** | A sniper rifle that can't hurt anyone | [Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack |
| **Non-Lethal Boxing Gloves** | "Pow!" | [Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack |
| **Non-Lethal Bomb** | "Boom!" | [Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack |
| **Water Gun** | "Splash!" | [Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack |

<small>Names and descriptions as shown in the game's ITEMS menu, checked on 12 October 2026.</small>

!!! info "Non-lethal weapons"

    According to the gamepass description, the weapons in the **[Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack** are only for show: you can't kill players with them, unlike the [troll items that kill](troll-items.md#items-that-kill).

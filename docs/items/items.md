# Items

Items are things you can hold and use. You open them with the **ITEMS** button in the menu, and the ones you carry appear in the hotbar at the bottom of the screen (up to 15 slots). Press **Equip?** to equip an item, and the button turns green and says **Equipped**.

The ITEMS menu has four sections:

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **[Troll items](#troll-items)** | Items you use on other players. Every use costs **Troll Tokens** |
| **[Gamepass items](#gamepass-items)** | Items that come with a [gamepass](gamepasses.md) |
| **[Normal items](#normal-items)** | Still to be written |
| **[Free community items](#free-community-items)** | Still to be written |

In [private servers](../servers/private.md), the owner can make all items free for everyone.

# Gamepasses

Gamepasses are bought once with Robux and are shared between the 18+ and the standard version. Click a card to open it on Roblox. This page **updates automatically**: prices and new gamepasses appear on their own.

!!! info "Buying in the standard version"

    The 18+ version lists a **Gamepass Info** notice: gamepasses can also be bought in the standard version, and they carry over.

# Gamepasses

Gamepasses are bought once with Robux. Click a card to open it on Roblox. This page **updates automatically**: prices and new gamepasses appear on their own.

Some gamepasses also give you an item in the **ITEMS** menu, like Fly, Invisibility, the coils and the non-lethal weapons: see [Gamepass items](items.md#gamepass-items).

!!! info "Gamepasses and the standard version"

    According to the game's description, gamepasses save between the 18+ and the standard version, and the 18+ version's **Gamepass Info** notice says that passes bought in the standard version carry over here. This hasn't been confirmed in-game yet: see [Game Versions](../game-info/versions.md#what-carries-over).

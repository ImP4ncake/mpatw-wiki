# Profiles and countries

The core idea of *Meet People Across The World* is that **everyone's country is visible**, so you always know where the people you are talking to come from.

## Your country

Each player is shown with their country. This is what makes it easy to start conversations like "Hi from Italy!" or to look for people from a specific place.

- The **Custom Country** gamepass lets players set any country as their origin. It is currently not for sale (see [Gamepasses](../economy/gamepasses.md)).
- The **Explorer** badge is awarded for discovering all **250 countries**, and it is one of the rarest badges in the game.

## Country Trivia

The game includes a **Country Trivia** activity. Reaching a streak of **50 correct answers** in a row gives the **Wayfarer** badge.

## Your profile

You can customize your profile, and other players can view it and leave [comments](reputation-and-comments.md) on it. Your profile also shows your [reputation](reputation-and-comments.md).

- The **Better Customization** gamepass unlocks extra customization options.
- Some badges unlock **titles** and **tags** you can show on your profile, for example **Invite a Friend!**, **Pet The Cat** and **Pet The Dog**.

## Cosmetics

Cosmetics come in different rarities. Unlocking a **Fabled** rarity cosmetic gives the **Fabled** badge.

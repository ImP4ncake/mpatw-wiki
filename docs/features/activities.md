# Activities

Besides chatting, the map is full of things to do. Many activities have a [badge](../badges.md) attached.

## Arenas

There are several combat arenas, each with its own weapon. Defeating **50 players** in an arena gives its badge.

| Arena | Badge |
|---|---|
| Sword Arena | Swordmaster |
| Gun Arena | Gunsmith |
| Boxing Arena | Boxer |
| Sniper Arena | Sniper |
| Bomb Arena | Arsonist |
| Greatsword Arena | Warrior |

!!! note

    The weapons in the **[Non-Lethal] Weapons Pack** gamepass are cosmetic only: they cannot defeat other players.

## Music and art

- **Piano**: play the piano to get the **Pianist** badge. It is one of the most common badges in the game.
- **Draw Area**: draw **1,000 times** to get the **Artist** badge.

## Games and challenges

- **Chess**: win **25 games** for the **Grandmaster** badge.
- **Country Trivia**: reach a **50 streak** for the **Wayfarer** badge. See [Profiles and countries](profiles-and-countries.md#country-trivia).
- **Lottery**: rolling **777** gives the **Win the Lottery** badge.
- **Leaderboards**: being **#1** on a non-timed leaderboard gives the **Angel** badge. There are also **Ranked Seasons** with an ELO rating that resets every month: finishing a season at #1 gives the **Sovereign** badge, which only one player had at the time of writing.

## Social activities

- **Drink Area**: serve **50 drinks** for the **Drink Maker** badge. The **Drink Maker** gamepass lets you make drinks.
- **AI Chatbot**: talk to it **50 times** for the **Robot** badge.
- **Trolls**: troll other players **50 times** for the **Troller** badge.
- **Gifting**: gift something to anyone for the **Gifter** badge.
- **Make it rain**: make it rain any currency for the **Rainmaker** badge.

## Other

- **Hammer**: build **10 blocks** with the Hammer for the **Builder** badge.
- **AFK World**: a place where you can earn coins while away. Earning **1,000 coins** there gives the **AFK** badge.
- **Pets**: there is a cat and a dog you can pet (**Pet The Cat** and **Pet The Dog** badges).

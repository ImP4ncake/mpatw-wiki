# Reputation and comments

## Reputation

Every player has a **reputation** score that other players can see. Reputation can go up, but it can also go **below zero**.

Two badges are tied to reputation:

| Badge | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Famous** | Reach **10,000** reputation |
| **Infamous** | Reach **-10,000** reputation |

Both are rare: far fewer players have Infamous than Famous.

## Comments

You can leave **comments** on other players' profiles, and they can leave comments on yours. Comments are a nice way to say thanks after a good conversation.

!!! warning "Be kind"

    Comments are public. Don't post personal information about anyone (including yourself), and don't use comments to harass people. Report abuse with Roblox's built-in tools.

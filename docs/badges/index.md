# Badges & Titles

A complete encyclopedia of achievements, unlockable titles and tag effects. Almost every badge unlocks a **title** and a **tag effect** (an emoji) that you can show off, and the **VIP subscription** gives [its own titles](titles.md#vip-titles). Everything here **updates automatically** from the official Roblox data.

??? info "How rarity is calculated"

    Rarity is calculated by the wiki: it compares how many players own a badge with how many own the **Welcome** badge (everyone who ever joined).

    | Rarity | Owned by |
    |---|---|
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--common">Common</span> | 25% of players or more |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span> | 2% to 25% |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--rare">Rare</span> | 0.2% to 2% |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--epic">Epic</span> | 0.01% to 0.2% |
    | <span class="mp-rarity mp-rarity--legendary">Legendary</span> | Less than 0.01% |

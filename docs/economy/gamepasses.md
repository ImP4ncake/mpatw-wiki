# Gamepasses

Gamepasses are bought once with Robux and are shared between the standard and the [18+ version](../versions.md) of the game.

!!! info "Prices"

    Prices and availability come from the game's public store listing (checked October 2026) and can change. Always check the price in the game before buying.

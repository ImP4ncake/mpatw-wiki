# Currencies

## Coins

**Coins** are the main in-game currency.

Ways to earn coins:

- **[Codes](codes.md)**: some codes give coins.
- **Invite a Friend!** badge: gives **100 coins** the first time.
- **AFK World**: you can earn coins while you are away. Earning 1,000 coins there gives the **AFK** badge.
- **Triple Coins** gamepass: triples the coins you earn from most sources (see [Gamepasses](gamepasses.md)).

## Lucky Tokens

**Lucky Tokens** are a second currency. At least one [code](codes.md) has given a Lucky Token as a reward.

## Spending and sharing

- **Gifting**: you can gift things to other players (the **Gifter** badge is awarded the first time).
- **Make it rain**: you can "make it rain" a currency, which gives the **Rainmaker** badge.
- **Tip Jar** gamepass: lets other players donate to you in-game.

!!! warning "Watch out for scams"

    Nobody can give you free Robux, and no one from the game will ever ask for your password. Never trade items outside the game's own systems.

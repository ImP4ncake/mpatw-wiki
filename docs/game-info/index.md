## How it started

**Nogo** first published *Meet People Across The World* as a social hangout where every player's country is visible, so you can meet and talk to people from all over the world, often with voice chat.

A month and a half later came the **18+ version**: a separate experience only for adults, with its own servers, coins and records. This wiki covers the 18+ version. The differences are on [Game Versions](versions.md).

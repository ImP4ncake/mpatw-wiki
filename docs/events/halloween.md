# Halloween

--8<-- "generated/event-status/halloween.md"

Halloween is the game's October event. While it runs, the game's name gets a 🎃 in front (**[🎃] 18+ Meet People Across The World**), and there are limited items and a limited badge to collect.

## Halloween 2026

| Edition | Halloween 2026 |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Running now |
| **Ends** | Around 2 November 2026 (from the in-game timer) |
| **Limited item** | Pumpkin Tip Jar |
| **Limited badge** | Trick or Treat! |

### What's new

- **Pumpkin Tip Jar**: a limited Halloween version of the tip jar. It is in the shop for the whole event and can be bought for yourself or sent as a **gift**. See [Limited Items](../items/limited-items.md).
- **Trick or Treat!** is back: win a special treat at the **Spooky Shed** to earn the limited badge. Hundreds of players are earning it every day of the event.

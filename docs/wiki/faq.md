# Wiki FAQ

??? question "Is this the official wiki?"

    No. It is a fan-made wiki, not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation or Nogo.

??? question "Why does it cover the 18+ version?"

    The wiki focuses on the 18+ version of the game. Most features are shared with the standard version, and the [Game Versions](../game-info/versions.md) page explains the differences.

??? question "How up to date is it?"

    Stats, badges, gamepasses and update dates refresh **automatically every hour** from the official Roblox data, and the official update announcements come straight from the game's Discord. Everything else is checked by hand and shows when it was checked.

??? question "Who writes it?"

    The wiki is made by [<span class="mp-face mp-face--owner"></span>ImP4ncake](suggest.md){ .mp-who }, with the information players send in.

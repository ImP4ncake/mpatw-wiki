# Wiki FAQ

??? question "Is this the official wiki?"

    No. It is a fan-made wiki, not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation or Nogo.

??? question "Why does it cover the 18+ version?"

    The wiki focuses on the 18+ version of the game. Most features are shared with the standard version, and the [Game Versions](../game-info/versions.md) page explains the differences.

??? question "How up to date is it?"

    Stats, badges, gamepasses and update dates refresh **automatically every hour** from the official Roblox data, and the official update announcements come straight from the game's Discord. Everything else is written by players and shows when it was checked.

??? question "Who writes it?"

    Players like you. See [How to Contribute](contribute.md).

??? question "I found a mistake. What do I do?"

    Click the edit button at the top of the page and fix it, or [open an issue](https://github.com/ImP4ncake/mpatw-wiki/issues/new) on GitHub.

# Wiki Guidelines

The rules that keep the wiki accurate and pleasant to read.

## Content rules

1. **Only add things you have checked.** Write what you saw in the game, not rumors. If you're not sure, say so or leave it out.
2. **Add dates to things that change.** Codes, prices and records go out of date: write when you checked them (for example "October 2026").
3. **No personal information.** Don't write about specific players, their countries or anything that could identify them. The only exception is the game's developer.
4. **No private staff material.** Only publish moderation information that is public. No internal documents, staff tests or leaks.
5. **No exploits or hacks.** Don't post ways to break the game's rules.
6. **Follow Roblox's rules.** The game is 18+, but the wiki follows the [Roblox Community Standards](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards): no explicit or hateful content.
7. **Use your own screenshots.** Crop them, keep them small (`.webp` or `.png`) and put them in `docs/assets/images/`.
8. **Write in English**, in a friendly and neutral tone.

## Encyclopedia pages and guides

The wiki keeps facts and advice apart:

- **Encyclopedia pages** (for example [Reputation](../mechanics/reputation.md)) explain objectively what something is and how it works.
- **Guides** (for example [Reputation Guide](../guides/reputation.md)) give practical tips and strategies.

Both link to each other, but each keeps its own purpose.

## Content status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Current** | Available in the game right now |
| **Limited** | Available only during an event |
| **Discontinued** | No longer obtainable |
| **Historical** | Older systems kept for the record |

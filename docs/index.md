# Meet People Across The World Wiki

Welcome to the **unofficial fan wiki** for *Meet People Across The World* (MPATW), the Roblox social hangout where you can see where everyone is from and talk to players from all over the world.

## Staying safe

MPATW is a social game where you talk to strangers, often with voice chat. A few reminders that apply to every social game on Roblox:

- Never share personal information such as your real name, address, school, phone number or passwords.
- Use Roblox's **block** and **report** tools if someone makes you uncomfortable.
- The game has its own rules, which are posted in its official community server.
